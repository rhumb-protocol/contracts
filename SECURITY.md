# Security

Report a vulnerability privately, to `security@rhumb.finance` or through GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting in any public Rhumb repository. Never in a public issue, a chat or a
social network; a report that arrives there is answered with the route and nothing else.

What we promise:

- An acknowledgement within 48 hours.
- A first assessment, with the severity, within 7 days.
- A fix, or the guardian's pause of the affected pools while one is made, as fast as the harm
  requires; withdrawals continue during a pause.
- Coordinated disclosure after the fix or after 90 days, whichever is first, unless you agree
  otherwise.
- No legal action against a researcher who followed the rules.

The rules of the bug bounty, with the scope and the awards, are at
https://rhumb.finance/security.
